About Me

Chemistry, applied with precision

From academic fundamentals to GMP-regulated production floors — a chemist trained to measure, verify and deliver.

GMP Environment QC Testing Class of 2025

I am a detail-oriented junior lab chemist and a 2025 graduate of Industrial & Applied Chemistry (B.Sc.) from Port Said University, Faculty of Science. My training goes beyond theory — it is grounded in chemical analysis, quality control and real manufacturing practice.

At Royal Cosmetics Company I worked inside a GMP-compliant production environment, following cosmetic products across the formulation lifecycle and running physicochemical tests — viscosity, pH stability and density — to confirm product consistency. A research placement in Cairo then broadened my analytical toolkit across paint chemistry, detergent chemistry and chromatography.

I take laboratory safety seriously, record data accurately and work well in a team. I am now seeking a junior lab chemist position in quality control or analytical testing, where precision and reliability matter every single day.
